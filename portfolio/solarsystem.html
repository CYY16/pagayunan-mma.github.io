Solar System

A 3D model of the solar system with all planets rendered in Blender.

Project Overview

This solar system model combines artistic visualization with scientific accuracy. Each celestial body is rendered in 3D with realistic proportions and orbital characteristics.

Tools Used

Blender 4.0

Render Time

Custom render settings

Resolution

1920 x 1080

Mercury

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun. It has no atmosphere and experiences extreme temperature swings.

Diameter 4,879 km
Distance from Sun 57.9 million km
Orbital Period 88 Earth days
Moons 0
Temperature -173°C to 427°C
Composition Iron core, silicate mantle

Fun Facts

  • Mercury has no atmosphere to retain heat
  • A day on Mercury lasts longer than its year
  • It has the most eccentric orbit of all planets

Venus

The hottest planet in our solar system due to its thick, toxic atmosphere.

Diameter 12,104 km
Distance from Sun 108.2 million km
Orbital Period 225 Earth days
Moons 0
Temperature 462°C (surface)
Composition Rocky with dense CO₂ atmosphere

Fun Facts

  • Venus rotates backwards compared to most planets
  • Its atmosphere is 96.5% carbon dioxide
  • Surface pressure is 92 times that of Earth

Earth

Our home planet. The only known planet to harbor life, with liquid water and a protective atmosphere.

Diameter 12,756 km
Distance from Sun 149.6 million km
Orbital Period 365.25 days
Moons 1 (The Moon)
Temperature -89°C to 58°C
Composition Rocky with 71% water coverage

Fun Facts

  • Earth is the only planet not named after a god
  • It has the largest moon relative to planet size
  • Earth's magnetic field protects us from solar radiation

Mars

The Red Planet, home to the largest volcano and canyon in the solar system.

Diameter 6,792 km
Distance from Sun 227.9 million km
Orbital Period 687 Earth days
Moons 2 (Phobos, Deimos)
Temperature -87°C to -5°C
Composition Rocky with iron oxide surface

Fun Facts

  • Mars has the largest volcano in the solar system (Olympus Mons)
  • It has seasons similar to Earth due to axial tilt
  • Mars has polar ice caps made of water and CO₂

Jupiter

The largest planet in our solar system. A gas giant with a Great Red Spot storm larger than Earth.

Diameter 142,984 km
Distance from Sun 778.5 million km
Orbital Period 12 Earth years
Moons 95+
Temperature -108°C (cloud tops)
Composition Mostly hydrogen and helium

Fun Facts

  • Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth
  • It acts as a 'cosmic vacuum cleaner' protecting inner planets
  • Jupiter has more mass than all other planets combined

Saturn

Famous for its spectacular ring system. Less dense than water, so it would float.

Diameter 120,536 km
Distance from Sun 1.43 billion km
Orbital Period 29 Earth years
Moons 146+
Temperature -139°C (cloud tops)
Composition Mostly hydrogen and helium

Fun Facts

  • Saturn would float in water if there was a bathtub big enough
  • Its rings are made mostly of ice particles
  • Titan, its largest moon, has lakes of liquid methane

Uranus

An ice giant that rotates on its side, making it unique among planets.

Diameter 51,118 km
Distance from Sun 2.87 billion km
Orbital Period 84 Earth years
Moons 27+
Temperature -197°C
Composition Water, methane, ammonia ices

Fun Facts

  • Uranus rotates on its side at a 98-degree angle
  • It was the first planet discovered with a telescope
  • Its blue-green color comes from methane in its atmosphere

Neptune

The windiest planet with speeds reaching 2,100 km/h, despite being farthest from the Sun.

Diameter 49,528 km
Distance from Sun 4.5 billion km
Orbital Period 165 Earth years
Moons 16+
Temperature -201°C
Composition Water, methane, ammonia ices

Fun Facts

  • Neptune has the fastest winds in the solar system
  • It was discovered through mathematical predictions
  • Triton, its largest moon, orbits backwards
